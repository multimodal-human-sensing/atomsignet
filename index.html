Research Overview

    Key Results

    From Raw Biosignals To An Affective Read In Three Steps

    Each physiological stream is treated as a sequence of irreducible atoms. The engine checks that every transition makes sense, repairs the ones that don’t, and scales its own reasoning to the difficulty of the interval.

    1. 01

      Atomize

      Per-sensor TCNs, cross-modal attention, and a Variational Information Bottleneck transform BVP, EDA, and temperature into compact physiological atoms that preserve affect-relevant autonomic dynamics while suppressing noise.

    2. 02

      Bridge

      The Markovian Reasoning Engine detects logic gaps and inconsistent multimodal transitions. Context-conditioned diffusion synthesizes bridge atoms to restore continuity, while high-integrity contradictions are retained as novel patterns for classifier fine-tuning.

    3. 03

      Scale

      At inference, Markovian test-time scaling adapts diffusion depth to contradiction severity, enabling targeted self-correction on difficult or noisy intervals without the memory overhead of conventional temporal models.

    Interactive Reasoning Console

    Contradictory Emotions Across Modalities

    The console reads the visual, acoustic and physiological streams separately and shows where they conflict instead of collapsing them into a single label.

    AtomSigNet: Reading Biosignal One Atom At A Time

    The wearable stream is broken into short atomic units. A diffusion model fills in atoms that don’t fit the sequence around them, and at test time the model keeps refining until the sequence holds together before the affect classifier makes its call.
